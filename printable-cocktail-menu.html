Drinks

Josh and Hena's house warming
Cocktails
Whiskey Sour
A classic. Bourbon balanced by sweetness and citrus.
To shaker: 15ml demerara syrup, 22.5ml lemon juice, 60ml bourbon.
Optional: Add egg white & dry shake.
Add ice, shake till cold (30s). Strain into glass with ice.
Bees' Knees
Smooth honey sweetness. Crowd favourite.
To shaker: 22.5ml honey syrup, 22.5ml lemon juice, 60ml gin, and ice. Shake till cold (30s). Strain into glass with ice.
Fancy Cocktail
Strong, rich, floral combination.
To glass: 1 barspoon demerara syrup, 7.5ml orange liqueur (rinse spoon into glass), 45ml bourbon, 2 dashes Angostura bitter, ice. Mix.
Daiquiri
Simple, sour, and strong.
To shaker: 15ml simple syrup, 22.5ml lime juice, 60ml rum. Shake till cold (30s). Strain into glass with ice.
Baby Guinness
Creamy, rich coffee shot.
Fill shot glass 2/3 full of Kahlua. Slowly pour Baileys over back of a spoon to float on top.
Long Cocktails
Gin Rickey
Cooling and refreshing.
To glass: 15ml lime juice, 45ml gin (or bourbon), ice. Top with soda water, stir gently.
Tom Collins
A gin and tonic, but sophisticated.
To glass: 15ml simple syrup, 22.5ml lemon juice, 45ml gin, ice. Top with soda water, stir gently.
Mocktails
Shirley Temple
Grenadine & lemonade.
Add 15ml grenadine to a glass of lemonade water and ice. Mix.
Lemon Lime Cooler
Citrus, syrup & soda.
Add 15ml lemon juice, 15ml lime juice, 15ml simple syrup to a glass of soda water and ice. Mix.
Orgeat Lemonade
Almond syrup & lemonade.
Add 30ml lemon juice and 15ml orgeat to a glass of lemonade and ice. Mix.
Grenadine Spritzer
Lime, grenadine & tonic.
Add 30ml lemon juice and 15ml grenadine to a glass of tonic water and ice. Mix.
Remember to rinse everything after use! Ask Josh or Hena for variations.
